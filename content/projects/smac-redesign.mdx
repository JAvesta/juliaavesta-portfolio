## The problem

SMAC&SHOP is SM Mall's mobile rewards and cashback app in the Philippines. Registration is key to customer acquisition, but its entry points operated in isolation, creating broken journeys.

<CalloutBox type="problem" title="Millions counted as acquired could not use the app">
4 of 5 legacy sign-up paths only created a **legacy loyalty record**, so the digital account never existed, pushing customers to re-register to get into SMAC&SHOP.
</CalloutBox>

## How we found out

We walked all five sign-up funnels as a customer: receipt QR, service desk, SMS shortcode, card sticker and the native app. Method: Walk-a-Mile field immersion, five journeys mapped in FigJam.

<CalloutBox type="insight" title="Three core friction patterns">
1. **The single-system trap:** most entry points created only a legacy card account.
2. **Redundant data entry:** people who registered in store filled in the same details again in the app.
3. **Security and trust barriers:** SMS shortcode links felt risky next to rising worry about phishing.
</CalloutBox>

## What shipped: one sign-up, two doors

<CalloutBox type="solution" title="Register in-person, scan, and you're set">
We kept mobile registration, simplified web and kiosk registration, and linked them with **OTP verification**: chosen for its low learning curve and higher trust than legacy text links.
</CalloutBox>

## Key takeaways

- **Fix the system, not five screens.** Patching each funnel would have kept five broken journeys.
- **Question what the metric counts.** "Acquired" cardholders still couldn't use the app.
- **Walk it as the customer.** That is where the drop-offs showed up, and why.
- **Choose the pattern people trust.** OTP beat pattern passwords and security questions.
