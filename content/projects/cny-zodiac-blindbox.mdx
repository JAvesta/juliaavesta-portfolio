## Leveling up an existing game

With Claude as a new tool under our belt, I wanted to make SMAC&SHOP's gamification features stand out.

SMAC&SHOP's Lunar New Year Blindbox in February 2026 rewarded newly acquired and current customers with points. It was a static, tap-to-play campaign: a video animation, then the prize arrives in an *angpao* (red packet).

## A hands-on blind box experience

To create a new, unique, joyous Lunar New Year feel, I wanted to integrate the following for 2027's campaign:

<CalloutBox type="solution" title="Unique prize unveiling">A ritual you take part in, not just a box you tap to open.</CalloutBox>
<CalloutBox type="solution" title="Filipino-Chinese traditions">Cultural aspects that show how the community celebrates.</CalloutBox>
<CalloutBox type="solution" title="Meaningful interactions">Every draw explains what the zodiac sign means.</CalloutBox>

## The concept: Chinese zodiac figurines

I chose this concept because the Chinese zodiac has a massive influence on Chinese culture, appearing throughout traditional folklore and still widely followed today (University of Washington, *The Chinese Zodiac*). At its core, each animal sign represents distinct characteristics, and people are believed to inherit the personality traits of their birth year's animal (China Highlights, *Chinese Zodiac*).

## Conceptualizing the user flow

1. **Light the incense:** an offering at the family altar.
2. **Strike the gong:** awaken your blessing.
3. **Meet your zodiac:** a golden figurine steps out.
4. **Unroll your fortune:** points or vouchers rewarded.

## Depth, moonlight and lanterns

- **Dark foreground, light background,** so the altar sits in front of the temple.
- **The moon as key light,** with highlights and cast shadows that follow the items hit by it.
- **Lanterns kept bright,** because New Year is all about the lights.

## Family altar ambience

1. **Prosperity bowl:** rice, eggs, coins and rolled bills. A lucky ritual meant to bring good fortune.
2. **Fruits and pineapple:** *ong lai*, the pineapple, means “fortune comes”, a symbol of wealth and positive energy.
3. **Incense sticks:** a spiritual bridge to honor deities and respect ancestors.
4. **Upside-down 福:** hanging the Chinese character upside down symbolizes the arrival of fortune.

## Unique prize unveiling

Instead of a simple tap, players must **light the incense sticks** and **tap a gong** to summon a random Chinese zodiac animal, which then holds their “fortune” or prize won.

## Every draw is a zodiac lesson

Each figurine's lore scroll covers **personality, lucky numbers, lucky colors and years born**, written about the people born in each year, the way families actually talk about their sign.

## Six steps from wish to prize

Play card → light match → light incense → bow 3 times → strike the gong → meet your zodiac → unroll the scroll → claim prizes.

## Reflection

- **Ritual over randomness.** Turning a blind box into a ritual made the reward feel earned. The cultural details carry the delight, not the prize alone.
- **Human-ideated, Claude-assisted.** I set the concept, flow and cultural direction; Claude helped me prototype it fast. The project was pitched and shelved, but it showed how quickly a gamified idea can reach a testable state.
