## Users confused upfront payment with autodebit

GoGym Philippines is a fitness app that lets members access gyms, workouts and flexible memberships.

<CalloutBox type="problem" title="Unexpected charges">
Transparent billing is vital for user trust. However, the lack of distinction between upfront and autodebit payments led to **unexpected charges** and a surge in support tickets for payment reversal.
</CalloutBox>

## Identifying the problem

I started from what members were already telling us. Support tickets showed people **confusing autodebit with one-time payments**, so I ran a heuristic evaluation of the packages and purchase details screens to find out why.

<CalloutBox type="insight" title="Signal → painpoint → insight">
Tickets showed **unintended subscriptions** → members were **surprised by monthly charges** → **the payment options were not clear**.
</CalloutBox>

### Heuristic evaluation

1. **No distinguishing UI for autodebit.** Put side-by-side with one-time payment membership options, the difference is not obvious.
2. **Unclear terms.** Membership duration is highlighted, every rate label is per month, and payment terms are difficult to read.
3. **Terminology needs validation.** Many customers may not know what “Autodebit” or “Upfront” means.

## A/B testing with 39 people

We made two versions of each screen and tested them in an onsite interview and survey with 39 people.

## Two new steps before payment

<CalloutBox type="solution" title="Intentional friction">
A confirmation bottom sheet and the autodebit terms and conditions now sit between choosing a package and reviewing the membership, so nobody signs up for a monthly charge by accident.
</CalloutBox>
